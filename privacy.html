Last updated: November 26, 2026

NitroTwixtor is a static HTML website. We do not require user accounts, logins, or registration, and we do not run a backend database to store personal profiles or payment information.

These third-party services operate independently and may collect IP addresses, device identifiers, or usage data under their own privacy policies when you play an embedded video or click a download link.

4. Children's Privacy

NitroTwixtor does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has contacted us with personal data, email us so we can delete it.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email support@nitrotwixtor.com or visit our Contact page.
